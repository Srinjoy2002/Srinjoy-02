01 / ABOUT

Curiosity, grounded
in engineering.

I am a PhD research scholar in Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, specialising in Control & Automation.

My work is motivated by a simple question: how can many relatively simple agents produce coordinated, robust and intelligent behaviour without a central controller?

I approach this through nonlinear and distributed control, graph-theoretic methods, collective animal behaviour and robotic experimentation. I am particularly interested in the connection between elegant mathematical models and systems that work outside simulation.
